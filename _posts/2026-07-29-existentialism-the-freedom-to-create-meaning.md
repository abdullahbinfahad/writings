Life doesn't arrive with a manual or a predefined purpose. Existentialism begins with this simple but powerful idea: meaning is not discovered it is created.


We are free to choose who we become, but that freedom comes with responsibility. Every decision, every action, and every moment shapes our identity. We cannot blame fate, society, or circumstances forever. At some point, we must decide what kind of person we want to be.

To me, existentialism is not about pessimism. It is about courage. It reminds us that uncertainty is a part of life, and instead of fearing it, we should use it as an opportunity to build a meaningful future.

The value of life is not measured by how long we live or how much we own, but by the purpose we create, the people we influence, and the legacy we leave behind. Meaning is not waiting for us somewhere in the future—it is built through the choices we make today.
