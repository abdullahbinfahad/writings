You are nothing but your own imagination. In other words, the only reason you perceive yourself as "you" is because your imagination creates that sense of identity. If your memories, mind, personality, and behavior were transferred into another body, the existence of your current self would effectively disappear. From this perspective, the individual has no truly independent physical identity. Consciousness itself has no fixed shape. It is fluid, free, and impossible to define with certainty.

Life is simply a state of responsibility entrusted to us by the Creator. Within it, we are given certain conditions, certain responsibilities, and the freedom to shape ourselves differently throughout different stages of our existence.

Everything is relative, and within that relativity, everything is ultimately meaningless. Life is painful, and that is its natural state. Nothing that we call normal or healthy exists above this instability. At the same time, life is not nearly as complicated as we make it seem.

What is truly remarkable is that humanity has imprisoned everything inside false definitions and now follows them almost like religion. We are trapped in a Rat Race built by ordinary people, yet we obey it as though it were absolute truth. The real problem is that we were never taught how to think. We were only taught what to think.

As a result, we continue worshipping a society designed by people who were no more certain than we are, without ever stopping to ask what we are actually doing or what life itself truly means.

Strangely enough, perhaps the greatest truth is this:

For some reason, we refuse to accept that we understand almost nothing.

Learn to see life simply.

Your time in this world is uncertain, and almost everything around you is unpredictable. There is no reason to make life unnecessarily complicated. Build a reality where you remain in control of yourself instead of losing yourself to the expectations of others.

And remember, nearly ninety-five percent of what you see around you is artificial.

Sadness is not an exception. It is a natural part of being human. Every person experiences it. In that sense, everyone is fundamentally equal. There is no rational reason to place anyone on a pedestal.

Yet we have been conditioned to think otherwise. Most of these beliefs were handed to us long before we ever questioned them, and many of us never even attempt to ask whether they are true.

If you honestly examine your own life, you will notice something interesting. Many of the promises you grew up believing, many of the expectations you carried into adulthood, never became reality. And still, despite all the evidence, you continue chasing the same illusions.

If that is true, can you honestly say you are in control of yourself?

We never learned how to respect ourselves. Instead, we spend our lives admiring the ambitions, achievements, and approval of others while neglecting our own potential.

Know your worth, and place it where it truly belongs.

And always remember:

A civilization is only worth following if it is built upon respect, dignity, and genuine humanity.
